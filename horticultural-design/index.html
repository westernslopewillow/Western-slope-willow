Design services

Container gardens for Western Colorado.

Thoughtful planting starts with the place: its light, elevation, seasons and the way you use it. We design container gardens and planting plans around those conditions.

Container garden design

From a pair of entrance containers to a terrace planting, we work with the scale of your space and the colors and textures you enjoy. Ask about an initial design, a seasonal refresh or an existing collection that needs a new direction.

Horticultural planning

For a broader property project, tell us about the location, exposure, existing planting and your goals. We can discuss the scope of a consultation and whether our services fit.

Where we work

We are based in Montrose and serve Telluride, Mountain Village, Ridgway, Ouray, Ophir and the surrounding Western Slope. Contact us to confirm availability for your location and schedule.

Start with a few details

Send the property location, photographs, approximate container sizes or planting area, and the season you have in mind. We will discuss the next step with you.
