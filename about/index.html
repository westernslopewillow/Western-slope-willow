Montrose, Colorado

Willow, grown with a purpose.

Western Slope Willow is our family business in Montrose, on Colorado's Western Slope. We grow specialty plants and offer willow for weaving, planting and project orders.

Talk to the person growing it

Hannah brings more than 20 years of horticultural experience and a horticulture degree from Oklahoma State University to the business. Our work includes growing willow and specialty plants, helping customers choose materials, and designing container gardens for Western Colorado properties.

Questions about a color, a bundle length or a planting project are welcome. Tell us what you are making or growing so we can help you choose from the options available.

Three ways to work with us

  • Basketry willow: dried rods and varieties for weaving and craft projects.
  • Willow plants and cuttings: living material for growing, with product-specific options in our Etsy shop.
  • Restoration inquiries: send the species, dimensions and timing your project requires so we can confirm whether our stock fits.

Buying from our shop

Our Etsy shop carries current retail options, prices and customer reviews. Shipping eligibility and dispatch estimates depend on the item and destination. For project quantities or a particular delivery date, contact Hannah before ordering.
