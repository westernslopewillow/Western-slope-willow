Named basketry willow

Whissender dried basketry willow

Whissender offers stout, bark-on rods for experienced weavers. Its olive-tan fresh color dries to warm brown-yellow tones, bringing a natural finish to woven work.

Get to know Whissender

Our Whissender listing identifies this willow as Salix triandra. These are naturally dried rods with the bark left on. They are stouter and less flexible than softer Black Maul willow, making material selection and preparation especially useful parts of planning your project.

If you are used to working with a softer willow, allow for that difference when choosing your pattern and the parts of the basket you intend to weave. Whissender is aimed at experienced weavers who are comfortable preparing their material.

Prepare before you weave

These dried rods need soaking and mellowing before use. Set aside preparation time before a class or planned weaving session. Follow the instructions for your purchase, or contact us if you need help deciding whether the material fits your project.
