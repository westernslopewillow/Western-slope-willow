Grow your own willow

Willow Plants and Cuttings

Choose willow for growing, from unrooted coyote willow cuttings to live trees and basketry plants. Start with the species and format that suit your plans.

Coyote willow cuttings

Our coyote willow, Salix exigua, listing offers 12-inch unrooted cuttings for propagation and landscaping. Each cutting needs to develop roots after planting. Check the listing for current bundle options, handling information and shipping details.

Check coyote willow cutting options on Etsy.

Peachleaf willow plants

Peachleaf willow, Salix amygdaloides, is a tree-form willow. Open the listing to review the plant sizes and options currently offered. If you are choosing material for a restoration project, send us the specified species, size and source requirements before ordering.

Check peachleaf willow options on Etsy.

Basketry and ornamental willow

The shop also carries live basketry and ornamental willow plants. These are useful starting points for a growing project when the named variety suits your site and intended use. Browse the Western Slope Willow Etsy shop for current varieties and plant formats.

For longer stems, our willow branch listing includes fresh-cut and dried options. Select the fresh-cut option when shopping for live material, and confirm the variety and timing with Hannah if those details matter to your project.

Know which format you are ordering

  • Unrooted cuttings: live stems that need to form roots after planting.
  • Rooted plants: plants with an existing root system; check each listing for the supplied size and container format.
  • Dried rods: material for basketry and crafts. Choose explicitly labeled live stock for growing.

For weaving materials, visit basketry willow. For a specified planting or construction project, visit willow for restoration.

Ask before a time-sensitive order

Send Hannah your destination, preferred species, quantity and planting date. She can check the current options and delivery timing. A 12-inch propagation cutting may not meet a project's live-stake specifications, so include required length and diameter when applicable.
